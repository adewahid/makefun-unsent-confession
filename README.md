## The entry

`main.ts` writes one two-part micro-confession to `/output/entry.txt`: a `To:` line naming who it was meant for, and the unsent message itself, typed exactly as it was once typed and never sent.

> To: Sara
>
> I typed "I still love you" the night you got engaged, then deleted it and sent "congrats!!" like someone who never knew you at all.

The whole entry is 141 characters (gate: 1–260), the message itself 131 characters (cap: 200). It is too late, not too clever: the engagement night is the one moment the words could have mattered, and the cheerful "congrats!!" is the mask that proves the sender knew exactly what he was burying. Swap the addressee and the whole thing breaks — only someone who knew Sara for years could pretend, in two exclamation marks, to be a stranger.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts   # writes /output/entry.txt
```
